# DR.Whisper

Hold a hotkey, talk, release: your words are transcribed, cleaned up and pasted wherever your cursor is, in English or Spanish. Made by [SP5 LLC](https://www.sp5llc.com).

## Download

**[Get the latest version](https://github.com/osebelen/dr-whisper-releases/releases/latest)**, then pick the installer for your PC:

| Your PC | File to download |
|---|---|
| Most Windows 10 / 11 PCs (Intel or AMD) | `DR.Whisper-Setup-<version>-x64.exe` |
| Windows on ARM (for example Surface Pro 11) | `DR.Whisper-Setup-<version>-arm64.exe` |

Not sure? Choose x64. It installs just for you (no administrator rights needed).

**Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen the first time.** DR.Whisper is not code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Before you start

DR.Whisper uses your own accounts, so you need two API keys (the app walks you through getting them the first time you open it):

- **ElevenLabs** turns your voice into text.
- **Anthropic (Claude)** cleans up the text and translates it.

Your voice and text go only to those two accounts. SP5 LLC never receives them. Usage is billed by those providers, not by SP5 LLC.

## Using it

| Hotkey | What you get |
|---|---|
| Hold `Ctrl` + `Win`, talk, release | English text |
| Hold `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `Win`, talk, release | Spanish text |

You can speak English, Spanish or a mix of both, whatever the output language. A quick tap of the hotkey keeps recording hands-free; press a hotkey again to finish. `Esc` cancels. Hotkeys, writing style, vocabulary and more are in Settings (click the tray icon).

## Updates

The app updates itself: it checks in the background and installs the new version the next time you restart it. You can check by hand under Settings, About, Updates. Turn on "Get beta versions early" there to try new versions sooner.
